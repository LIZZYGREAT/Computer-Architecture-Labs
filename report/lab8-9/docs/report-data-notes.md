## Expected versus observed

- `expected_*` values in `data/wave-capture-log.csv` come from the test image/ELF, course checks or the directed interrupt testbench.
- `observed_time_window` and `observed_values` are blank. They must be entered from the corresponding Vivado Wave after opening the listed WDB and WCFG.
- The Python figure `figures/regression_metrics.png` plots functional-point counts, pipeline cycles and stall cycles from the final `result.txt` files. The six course images have different program lengths, so the cycle values are not a performance comparison.
- The test-source/ELF PC anchors are cross-referenced in `docs/wave-capture-handoff.md` and the CSV context column. No screenshot observation is inferred from a trace or expected value.
